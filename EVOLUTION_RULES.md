# Balatromon Evolution Rules

- Ordinary Digivolution requires full Bond and a compatible Digivice.
- Standard routes remain available whenever the Digimon is not in a Care Crisis.
- Hunger-based and rough-care routes add extra choices to the interactive branch panel.
- D-3 specifically unlocks the Armor routes: Pegasusmon, Nefertimon, Flamedramon, and Halsemon.
- At 3 Care Mistakes, normal routes are suppressed. A marked bad route is forced/selected instead.
- If no bad route exists at 3 Care Mistakes, a Digivice De-Digivolves the Digimon to its Fresh form. If a shop-bought Digimon has no personal history, all reachable Fresh ancestors are offered. Standalone high-stage forms with no incoming line use one deterministic random Fresh form as an emergency reset.
- Resolving a Care Crisis resets Care Mistakes to 0; Hunger is preserved, so a still-hungry Digimon can begin accumulating mistakes again.
